Repbeat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Repbeat is a workout voice navigator made by Tesselex Software Inc. This policy explains what data the app handles and what happens to it. The short version: your workouts live on your device, an optional account syncs them, and we don't track you, profile you, or sell anything about you.

Data stored on your device

Your weekly plan, workout history, settings, profile photo, coach audio cache, and the optional body profile from onboarding (bodyweight, sex, and training experience, used only to estimate starting weights) are stored locally on your iPhone. The app works fully offline. Heart rate and calorie figures measured by an Apple Watch during a workout are shown in your session summary and stored only on your devices — they are never transmitted to our servers.

Data we collect with an account (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Apple, we store the following on our backend (hosted on Supabase) to provide sync and the studio coach voice:

  • Account identifiers — your Apple-provided sign-in identifier and, if shared, your email address (you can hide it with Apple's private relay).
  • First name — the name you enter in the app, used so your coach can address you.
  • Workout data — your plan and completed-session logs (exercises, sets, durations), synced across your devices.
  • App settings — your preferences (coach, workout mode, music source, and, if you connect Apple Music, the name of the playlist you picked), synced across your devices. Your body profile from onboarding is not synced.

Coach voice lines (short workout phrases that may include your first name) are generated through the ElevenLabs text-to-speech API and cached. No other personal data is sent to any third party.

Subscriptions

Repbeat Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We never see your payment details.

Apple Music

If you choose Apple Music as your music source, Repbeat asks for permission to access your Apple Music library so it can list your playlists and play them during workouts. Playback happens through Apple's own music player. We do not collect your listening history; the only thing stored outside your device is the name of the playlist you picked, as part of synced settings if you have an account.

Health data

With your permission, the Apple Watch app reads heart rate and active energy during workouts via HealthKit, and saves your workout to Apple Health. HealthKit data is used only for the features described above, stays within Apple's Health framework and your devices, and is never shared with our servers, used for advertising, or disclosed to third parties.

No tracking

Repbeat contains no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no trackers. We do not sell or share your data with data brokers, and we do not use your data for advertising.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all synced data at any time from Profile → Delete account inside the app. This permanently removes your account, synced plans, and workout history from our servers. Data stored locally on your device is removed when you delete the app.
