Setway — Support

Setway navigates your strength workout the way GPS navigates a drive: music plays, and your coach's voice lowers it to announce sets, rests, and what's next — hands-free.

Common questions

  • Can I use my own music app? Yes — pick "I'll play my own music" in the Music tab. Spotify, YouTube Music, podcasts — anything playing on your phone works; Setway lowers it to speak and brings it right back.
  • Apple Music says it can't connect. Apple Music playback inside Setway requires an active Apple Music subscription and permission (Settings → Setway → Media & Apple Music).
  • The coach isn't speaking. Check the ring/silent switch and volume, and that a workout is running. Studio voices download after sign-in — the Coaching tab shows preparation progress.
  • How do I end a set early? Tap "Done early — next", or in Motion mode just rack the weight — your Apple Watch detects the set ending.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Profile → Setway Pro → Manage, or Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. You keep Pro until the period ends.
  • How do I delete my account? Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your synced data from our servers.
